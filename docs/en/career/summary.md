# Valeriy Veselov — iOS Developer

## Profile

iOS developer with a contribution history at OneTwoTrip spanning December 2021 to September 2026. Experienced in developing and maintaining a large modular travel application, shared UI components, end-to-end customer journeys, automated checks and iOS delivery tooling. Contributed to both OneTwoTrip and Solar.

The dates refer to the engineering history covered in this portfolio.

- **Solar:** contributed to a separate app built on a shared codebase, including onboarding, authentication, profile, loyalty and digital collectibles.
- **Shared UI:** developed and integrated configurable calendar and UI components across product areas; contributed to UI 2.0 migrations.
- **Support and account flows:** worked on the custom support chat, reusable authentication, notification settings, profile, cashback and virtual-card interfaces.
- **Quality:** wrote and maintained unit, snapshot and UI tests; investigated regressions and improved testability of product flows.
- **CI and engineering tooling:** contributed to Jenkins tools for dSYM handling, agent maintenance, build diagnostics and repository metrics, including retryable metric publication and historical analysis.
